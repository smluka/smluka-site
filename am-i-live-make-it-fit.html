am I live: make it fit

Designing and building props, movements, positions and scenes in a direct, intuitive colaborative process with the artist. The first input for scenography design from the artist was six words with no particular order: holy, authority, machine, speech, manifesto, poetry. Additionally, we decided to use only what is present in the building, including the leftover material in the workshop. I focused on symbolism through shapes - triangles, circles, colours and language, importance, authority through movements. I wanted the podium from which the speech was delivered to move and emphasise the speech through its perception. For stronger delivery the podium would lean forward with the body of the performer. The cut-outs in its front emphasize the hand gestures of the speaker.
