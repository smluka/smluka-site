limbo lounge

Limbo lounge is a waiting place, a meeting place - an inbetween place consisting of three intertwining pieces of unclear identity. Are they benches, chairs, tables, bartables? Is it a table because it goes over a chair or is it a chair because it goes under another table, or is that thing a chair after all? Are they all at once, and in that case what do you call something like that?

Limbo Lounge is a usable sculpture that smudges the hierarchical lines that furniture pieces impose on each other. It smudges furniture’s identity that we assume it has based on our ingrained domestic knowledge. It suggests function through its materials - objects once used in domestic space, of familiar shape, that no longer needed to serve their purpose.
